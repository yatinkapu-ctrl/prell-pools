About Prell Pools

Pete Prell and 20 years of pool water.

Pete is the mastermind behind Prell Pool Service & Repair, with more than 20 years in the swimming pool industry. He runs it from Chester County alongside his wife and daughters, who have been part of successful local businesses here for over a decade.

The team has grown with a small group of hard working, trustworthy people, so the level of service stays high and the pricing stays reasonable. We would love to answer your questions and help make your backyard the best part of the summer.

  • Free phone support. Troubleshooting advice at no charge, customer or not.
